TalkTV

Video meetings on Apple TV

Meetings at a glance

See upcoming Nextcloud Talk meetings from your calendar and join from your TV.

Made for the big screen

View participant video streams with automatic active-speaker focus and manual participant selection.

Audio and network camera

Use a compatible Bluetooth microphone and an H.264 RTSP network camera for outgoing video. Preview the camera in Settings.

Free or Pro

All features are available free, with a 30-minute limit per meeting connection. A one-time TalkTV Pro purchase enables unlimited meeting durations.

Requirements

Requires an existing compatible Nextcloud Talk server with high-performance signaling. TalkTV is an independent client and is not published by Nextcloud GmbH.
