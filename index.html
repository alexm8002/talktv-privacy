Privacy Policy

How TalkTV handles account credentials, meetings, audio, video and in-app purchases.

Last updated: October 17, 2026

TalkTV is an independent video conferencing application for Apple TV that connects to servers compatible with Nextcloud Talk.

1. Account and authentication

TalkTV uses the server address, username, and application password provided by the user to connect to their Nextcloud server. Credentials are stored locally using Apple's secure storage mechanisms, including Keychain.

2. Meetings and calendars

The application accesses calendar information and scheduled meetings from the configured server. This information is used to display meetings and enable users to join them.

3. Audio and video

TalkTV uses the selected microphone and, when configured, an RTSP network camera for video conferences. Audio and video streams are transmitted to meeting participants through the communication infrastructure of the configured Nextcloud server.

The application also provides local microphone and camera testing functionality.

4. In-app purchases

TalkTV Pro purchases are managed by Apple through StoreKit. Payments are processed by Apple. TalkTV verifies Pro entitlements without requesting users' payment card information.

5. Storage, transmission, and security

Certain preferences and information required for the application's operation are stored locally on Apple TV. Communications with the configured server are subject to that server's infrastructure and privacy practices.

Audio and video communications use standard WebRTC encryption protocols. Local RTSP camera streams may be unencrypted depending on camera configuration.

6. Data retention and deletion

Users may remove the application and its associated local data. Data stored on the Nextcloud server is managed by the server administrator or service provider.

7. Third-party services

TalkTV uses Apple services for in-app purchases and third-party software components for audio and video communications. Any data processing carried out by these services must be reflected in the application's privacy disclosures.

TalkTV is an independent application and is not developed or published by Nextcloud GmbH.

TalkTV Pro wird über Apples StoreKit erworben. Die Zahlungsabwicklung erfolgt durch Apple. TalkTV überprüft die Pro-Berechtigung, ohne Bank- oder Kreditkartendaten anzufordern.
